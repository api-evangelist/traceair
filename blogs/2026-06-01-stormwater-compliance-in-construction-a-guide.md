IN SIMPLE TERMS A Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) is the document a construction site must file under the Clean Water Act once one acre or more of land is disturbed. It lists the best management practices (BMPs), inspection schedule, and responsible parties. Compliance, however, is not the document.
