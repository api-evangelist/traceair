In early August 2026, Dream Finders Homes agreed to acquire Beazer Homes for $33.50 a share in cash, giving the transaction an enterprise value of roughly $2.2 billion. If completed, the combination would create the country's sixth-largest homebuilder, operating in 26 markets and approximately 520 active communities.
